How to Play

  • 🃏 Flip two cards at a time. Same picture = a match!
  • ✅ Correct match: +10. Wrong match: -2 (score never goes below 0).
  • ❤️ You have 3 lives in every level.
  • 🦠 Virus card: -10 points and lose 1 life (starts in Level 2).
  • ⭐ Bonus card: +20 points (starts in Level 3).
  • ⏱️ Level 3 and the Final Challenge have a timer.
  • 🏁 At the end: +25 per life left and +1 per second left.
  • 🎖️ Ranks: File Rookie, File Hunter, Memory Agent, Memory Master.
